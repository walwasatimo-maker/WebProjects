OUR STORY

Origin:
This hub started in 2015 by the family of Mr. Waiswa Robert of Kitumbugulu, Butamira Ward, Buyengo towncouncil in Jinja district. It started as a small personal business but later expanded due to the high demand of its products by the surrounding community.
Why it was started:
The hub was started to equip the local community with computer skills and proving all computer related services.
Our growth:
Since its official opening in 2015, the hub has been growing at a very terrible speed due to the available market for the products produced and the high need to acquire computer skills by the surrounding community.
