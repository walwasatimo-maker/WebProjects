About me

Origin:

I was born on 1st July in the village of Kitumbugulu, Butamira ward, Buyengo Town Council in Jinja district. I am a son to Mr. Waiswa Robert and Mrs. Rose Waiswa.

I originated from the Abaise Igaga Clan one of the strong sounding clans in the Abasoga tribe in Busoga sub-region in the Eastern part of Uganda. Our totem is the Buffallo commonly known as "Embogo" in Lusoga language

When there, I went to Rural Students Learning Computer in Uganda and attained a Certificate in computer ie MS word, Excel, Presentation and Webdesign.
Started University education at Kampala International University (KIU), pursuing a Bachelors of Science in Computer Science.
