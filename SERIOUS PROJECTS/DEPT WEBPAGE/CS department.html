About the CS Department

The CS department was initiated at this university in 2019 due to the need of computer knowledge by many of the University students by then. It started with a small number of students but later due to the high demand of computer skills in the job field, the number went on growing.

As it stands, the University department enrollment is at 800 students and we hope this number to grow bigger in a five year period time to about 2000. To archieve all that, it has been God on our side but not our own making.

ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

A applicant applying for any of our university courses should have obtained the minimum university admission REQUIREMENTS for a particular program as indicated below:

  1. Computer Science
    • Masters

      • Should have obtained atleast a second class degree upper at bachelor level in a related field from a recognised university or any institution of learning recognised by Walwasa Everywhere University.

    • Bachelor

      • Should have obtained atleast two principle passes at A'level obtained at the same sitting of which they must be in Mathematics Physics, and atleast a subsidiary in Economics, Entrepreneurship, Biology, etc.

    • Diploma

      • The applicant should have atleast a principle pass in Physics, Mathematics and a subsidiary pass in Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Agriculture, etc.

  2. Software Engineering
    • Masters

      • Should have obtained atleast a second class degree upper at bachelor level in a related field from a recognised university or any institution of learning recognised by Walwasa Everywhere University.

    • Bachelor

      • Should have obtained atleast two principle passes at A'level obtained at the same sitting of which they must be in Mathematics Physics, and atleast a subsidiary in Economics, Entrepreneurship, Biology, etc.

    • Diploma

      • The applicant should have atleast a principle pass in Physics, Mathematics and a subsidiary pass in Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Agriculture, etc.

  3. Information Technology
    • Masters

      • Should have obtained atleast a second class degree upper at bachelor level in a related field from a recognised university or any institution of learning recognised by Walwasa Everywhere University.

    • Bachelor

      • Should have obtained atleast two principle passes at A'level obtained at the same sitting of which they must be in ANY A'level science combination with credits in Mathematics and English at O'level obtained at the same sitting.

    • Diploma

      • The applicant should have atleast a principle pass in ANY A'level science combination with credits in Mathematics and English at O'level obtained at the same sitting.

  4. Computer Forensics
    • Masters

      • Should have obtained atleast a second class degree upper at bachelor level in a related field from a recognised university or any institution of learning recognised by Walwasa Everywhere University.

    • Bachelor

      • Should have obtained atleast two principle passes at A'level obtained at the same sitting of which they must be in Mathematics Physics, Geography, History and atleast a subsidiary in Economics, Entrepreneurship, Biology, etc.

    • Diploma

      • The applicant should have atleast a principle pass in Physics, Mathematics, Geography, History and a subsidiary pass in Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Entrepreneurship, Agriculture, etc.

  5. Library Management
    • Masters

      • Should have obtained atleast a second class degree upper at bachelor level in a related field from a recognised university or any institution of learning recognised by Walwasa Everywhere University.

    • Bachelor

      • Should have obtained atleast two principle passes at A'level obtained at the same sitting of which they must be in ANY A'level combination and atleast a credit in Mathematics and English at O'level obtained at the same sitting

    • Diploma

      • The applicant should have atleast a principle pass in ANY A'level combination and atleast a credit in Mathematics and English at O'level obtained at the same sitting.
